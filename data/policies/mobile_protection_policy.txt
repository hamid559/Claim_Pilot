MOBILE DEVICE PROTECTION POLICY
Policy Version: 2026.1

========================================
SECTION 1 — ELIGIBILITY
========================================

1.1 Covered Devices

Coverage applies to enrolled smartphones and tablets purchased
from an authorized retailer and registered under an active
protection plan.

1.2 Active Coverage

A device must have an active protection plan on the date the
damage or loss occurs.

1.3 Waiting Period

Claims submitted within 14 days of initial enrollment are not
eligible unless the protection plan explicitly waives the waiting
period.

========================================
SECTION 2 — ACCIDENTAL DAMAGE
========================================

2.1 Accidental Physical Damage

Accidental physical damage caused by an unexpected external event
is covered when the device is enrolled in an active protection plan.

Examples include:

- Dropping the device
- Accidentally cracking the screen
- Accidental impact with a hard surface

2.2 Functional Damage

Damage that prevents normal device operation may qualify for
repair or replacement subject to the applicable plan limits.

2.3 Cosmetic Damage

Minor scratches, dents, discoloration, or other cosmetic
deterioration that does not affect device functionality are not
covered as accidental damage.

========================================
SECTION 3 — LIQUID DAMAGE
========================================

3.1 Liquid Damage Coverage

Liquid damage is covered only when the customer's protection plan
explicitly includes liquid damage protection.

3.2 Exclusion

If liquid damage protection is not included in the customer's plan,
liquid-related damage is excluded from coverage.

========================================
SECTION 4 — EXCLUSIONS
========================================

4.1 Intentional Damage

Damage caused intentionally by the customer is not covered.

4.2 Unauthorized Repairs

Damage resulting from repairs or modifications performed by an
unauthorized service provider is not covered.

4.3 Normal Wear and Tear

Normal deterioration resulting from ordinary use is not covered.

4.4 Lost or Stolen Devices

Lost or stolen devices are not covered under the accidental damage
protection described in this policy.

========================================
SECTION 5 — CLAIM REQUIREMENTS
========================================

5.1 Required Documents

A customer submitting a damage claim must provide:

- Proof of purchase
- Device IMEI or serial number
- Description of the incident
- Photographs showing the damage

5.2 Additional Information

The claims team may request additional documentation when the
available information is insufficient to determine eligibility.

========================================
SECTION 6 — CLAIM LIMITS
========================================

6.1 Repair or Replacement

Approved claims may result in device repair or replacement depending
on the protection plan and assessment of the damage.

6.2 Deductible

A deductible may apply to an approved claim depending on the
customer's protection plan.

========================================
SECTION 7 — CLAIM REVIEW
========================================

7.1 Eligibility Assessment

Claim eligibility is determined using the customer's active plan,
the reported incident, the type of damage, and applicable policy
coverage.

7.2 Human Review

A claim must be escalated to human review when:

- Relevant policy information is missing.
- The customer's coverage cannot be determined.
- Evidence conflicts with the reported incident.
- The claim involves circumstances not addressed by the policy.

7.3 No Unsupported Decisions

The claims assistant must not invent policy rules or make an
eligibility determination when sufficient policy evidence is not
available.
